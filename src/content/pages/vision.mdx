<Section eyebrow="Our vision">

Pine Lake Covenant isn’t here to be the church the world expects us to be, but the community God is calling us to be. The vision is short — _to find and follow Jesus, together_ — and it takes a lifetime to live.

That happens in two directions:

- **We gather** — to worship, to learn, and to be known by name rather than lost in a crowd.
- **We’re sent** — back into the neighborhoods, schools, and workplaces of the Sammamish plateau, carrying the good news of Jesus into ordinary weeks.

</Section>
