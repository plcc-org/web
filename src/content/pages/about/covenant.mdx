<Section eyebrow="Why a denomination?" heading="Accountable, supported, connected">

For us, being part of a denomination isn’t about being institutional — it’s about being accountable, supported, and connected. The Covenant provides shared theology, pastoral care and training, and partnership in mission, both locally and globally.

</Section>

<Section eyebrow="What the Covenant emphasizes" heading="A few long-standing convictions">

A combination of historic Christian orthodoxy and a warm, lived faith. These are the same convictions we hold at Pine Lake — you can read ours in [What We Believe](/beliefs/).

</Section>

<Callout heading="A posture of unity and grace">

One distinctive of the Covenant is our desire to focus on what unites followers of Jesus rather than what divides us. Where faithful Christians may differ on secondary issues, we aim to be thoughtful, generous, and grounded — keeping Jesus at the center.

</Callout>

<Section eyebrow="Connected beyond Pine Lake" heading="Part of a global family">

Being Covenant connects Pine Lake to churches across the U.S. and around the world, as well as missionaries and partner organizations. It also provides oversight and support for our pastors and leaders.

</Section>
