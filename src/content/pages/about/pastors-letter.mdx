When my wife Ashley and I moved our three kids—Ellie, Gwen, and Stephen—from Austin, Texas to Sammamish, we didn’t just move for a job. We moved because we felt called to be part of a community that was trying to live out something real.

We’re what you might call “front yard people.” We love being outdoors, whether it’s family bike rides, rock climbing, or just hosting a bonfire with neighbors. For us, faith isn’t just something that happens inside a building; it’s about how we show up for each other in practical, creative ways.

At Pine Lake, we don’t pretend to have all the answers. Life is complicated, and faith often feels more like a slow, steady journey than a destination. We’re simply a group of people trying to be a beacon of hope in a broken world — learning to be with Jesus, become like Jesus, and do what Jesus did.

Whether you’ve been following Jesus for a lifetime or you’re not even sure you have a faith journey, you are welcome here. We care about being a place where family means more than just a demographic, and where community is more than just a social circle. It’s about belonging.

You don’t have to have it all figured out to join us. Come however you’re comfortable — whether you’re all-in or still cautious — and you’ll find people glad to walk with you. We’re often gathered around coffee or food, and we’d love for you to join the conversation.

If you’re looking for a place to explore faith at your own pace, we’d love to have you join us on a Sunday morning.

</Letter>
