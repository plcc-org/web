      ## What is Stephen Ministry?

      Stephen Ministry is a confidential, one-to-one care relationship. Trained volunteers (called Stephen Ministers) meet regularly with individuals who are going through a difficult time.

      This might include grief, illness, caregiving stress, divorce, job loss, or other life transitions. Stephen Ministers are not counselors or therapists — they are caring listeners who offer presence, encouragement, and support.

      ## Who is this for?

      Anyone carrying something heavy — grief, illness, caregiving, divorce, job loss — who’d be helped by a steady listener. You don’t need to be a member of Pine Lake, or to have any church background.

      ## What does care look like?

      A Stephen Minister meets with one person at a time, usually on a weekly basis, for as long as support is needed. These conversations are confidential and focused on listening, encouragement, and practical care.

      Stephen Ministers go through extensive training before they’re matched with anyone, and are supervised throughout.

      ## How to get connected

      If you think Stephen Ministry might be helpful — for yourself or someone you care about — reach out. A coordinator will follow up privately and help you work out whether it’s a good fit.
