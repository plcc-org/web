<Section heading="What is Stephen Ministry?">

Stephen Ministry is a confidential, one-to-one care relationship. Trained volunteers (called Stephen Ministers) meet regularly with individuals who are going through a difficult time.

This might include grief, illness, caregiving stress, divorce, job loss, or other life transitions. Stephen Ministers are not counselors or therapists — they are caring listeners who offer presence, encouragement, and support.

</Section>

<Section heading="Who is this for?">

Stephen Ministry is for anyone who would benefit from steady, personal support during a challenging season. You don’t need to be a member of Pine Lake, and there’s no expectation that you already have a church background.

- Those experiencing grief or loss
- People facing illness or caregiving responsibilities
- Individuals navigating major life changes
- Anyone feeling overwhelmed or isolated

</Section>

<Section heading="What does care look like?">

A Stephen Minister meets with one person at a time, usually on a weekly basis, for as long as support is needed. These conversations are confidential and focused on listening, encouragement, and practical care.

Stephen Ministers receive extensive training and ongoing supervision to ensure care is thoughtful, respectful, and trustworthy.

</Section>
