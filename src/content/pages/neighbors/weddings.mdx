<Split image="../../assets/images/467727902_18149338249338056_7861795867212358283_n.jpg" alt="A light-filled room at Pine Lake, set up with chairs for a ceremony" eyebrow="Celebrate" heading="Weddings" tone="paper">

Weddings are a significant moment of transition and commitment. Our Wedding Coordinator is available to support you through planning, rehearsal, and the wedding day, including:

- Verifying availability of wedding support staff and requested services
- Working with pastoral staff to help direct the rehearsal
- Being present at the wedding (and reception, if held at Pine Lake)
- Assisting the wedding party prior to the ceremony
- Helping oversee on-site details at the church

To inquire about a wedding at Pine Lake, call [(425) 392-8636](tel:+14253928636) or email [office@plcc.org](mailto:office@plcc.org).

</Split>

<Split image="../../assets/images/plcc-prayer-candles.jpg" alt="Candles lit for a reflective moment of remembrance" eyebrow="Remember" heading="Memorials" reverse={true} tone="sand">

When a loved one dies, there is a need to commemorate their life and memory — and to find hope and comfort in the midst of grief. Our Memorial Team provides support for memorial services held at Pine Lake.

Our Memorial Coordinator can assist with coordinating the service, scheduling participants, and arranging an after-service reception when appropriate, working closely with pastoral staff to offer support and direction.

For memorial service inquiries, call [(425) 392-8636](tel:+14253928636) or email [office@plcc.org](mailto:office@plcc.org).

</Split>
