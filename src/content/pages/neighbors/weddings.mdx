<Split image="/assets/images/467727902_18149338249338056_7861795867212358283_n.jpg" eyebrow="Celebrate" heading="Weddings" tone="paper">

Weddings are a significant moment of transition and commitment. Our Wedding Coordinator is available to support you through planning, rehearsal, and the wedding day, including:

- Verifying availability of wedding support staff and requested services
- Working with pastoral staff to help direct the rehearsal
- Being present at the wedding (and reception, if held at Pine Lake)
- Assisting the wedding party prior to the ceremony
- Helping oversee on-site details at the church

To inquire about a wedding at Pine Lake, call [(425) 392-8636](tel:+14253928636) or email [office@plcc.org](mailto:office@plcc.org).

</Split>

<Split image="/assets/images/plcc-prayer-candles.jpg" eyebrow="Remember" heading="Memorials" reverse={true} tone="sand">

When someone you love dies, there is suddenly a lot to arrange — at exactly the moment you have the least room for it. Our Memorial Team carries as much of that as they can for services held at Pine Lake.

Our Memorial Coordinator can assist with coordinating the service, scheduling participants, and arranging a reception afterwards if you’d like one, working closely with our pastors throughout.

For memorial service inquiries, call [(425) 392-8636](tel:+14253928636) or email [office@plcc.org](mailto:office@plcc.org).

</Split>
