<Section heading="What is Meals of Care?">

Meals of Care provides short-term meal support for individuals and families going through a challenging season. This might include a recent surgery, serious illness, the arrival of a new baby, or a time of grief or loss.

Meals are prepared and delivered by members of the Pine Lake community as a simple expression of care and support.

</Section>

<Section heading="Who is this for?">

Meals of Care is for anyone who would benefit from a few days or weeks of practical help. You don’t need to be a member of Pine Lake to receive care.

- Individuals or families facing illness or recovery
- Those grieving the loss of a loved one
- Families welcoming a new child
- People navigating a particularly demanding season of life

</Section>

<Section heading="How it works">

Meal support is typically offered for a limited period of time, based on availability and need. We do our best to be thoughtful and respectful of dietary needs and schedules.

If you’re unsure whether Meals of Care is a good fit, you’re welcome to reach out and ask — we’re happy to talk it through.

</Section>

<Section heading="How to request meals">

To request Meals of Care — for yourself or on behalf of someone else — please contact the church office. A member of our care team will follow up privately.

</Section>
