<Section heading="What care usually looks like">

Care here is simple and human: a conversation, prayer if that’s meaningful to you, practical help when life is heavy, and connection to people who can walk with you for a while.

- Pastoral care and prayer
- Meals and practical support during illness or crisis
- Support groups, including an Alzheimer’s caregivers group
- Care and presence around memorial services and times of loss

</Section>
