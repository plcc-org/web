Most of what makes Pine Lake feel like home happens outside of Sunday — a smaller group that meets through the week, a shared meal, or the people who show up when a season turns hard.

Some groups form for a season at a time: six or ten people sharing meals, conversation, a short study and prayer, with new ones starting three times a year. Others run the length of a school year, September to May, and go deeper for having stayed together longer.

Others form around something other than a study:

- **Worship Community Night** — bring an instrument and play through a set alongside the band and A/V team, with snacks and time to pray together.
- **Hiking Group** — one Saturday a month, out on a trail.
- **Friends With Yarn** — knitting and crochet around a table a couple of Saturdays a month.
- **Anchor** — women gathering twice a month, around hope, faith and friendship.
- **Coffee Break** — women meeting every other Monday morning for conversation and connection.

The social groups often include people from the wider community who’ve never been to a service here. [MomCo](/momco/) works much the same way.

When life is difficult, there’s [prayer, care, and practical support](/neighbors/care/) here too — you don’t have to walk alone.

<Split image="/assets/images/500083306_18167175181338056_8168148552855409791_n.jpg" alt="People sharing a meal and conversation together" heading="Tuesdays Together" eyebrow="Dinner, twice a month" reverse={true} tone="sand">

Twice a month there’s dinner at church, and everyone’s invited. A main dish is provided — burgers, hot dogs, tacos — and people bring a side or a dessert to share if they can.

It’s one of the few things here that isn’t organized around a life stage or a shared interest — just the church at the same tables. It’s free, it runs through the program year, and there are seasonal gatherings alongside the dinners.

Following Jesus pulls us outward. Nearby, that mostly looks like turning up alongside organizations already doing the work — stocking a food bank, helping a neighbor through a sudden financial crisis, sending handmade cards and gifts in to young people at a center close by.

Each spring we host the Global 6K for Water alongside World Vision and another Covenant church — a walk that helps provide clean water.

[See who we partner with locally →](/neighbors/serve/)

</Split>

<Split image="/assets/images/481269178_1059869906180998_2732338907948607869_n.jpg" alt="The church gathered in matching shirts for the Global 6K for Water walk" heading="Partners around the world" eyebrow="Further afield" reverse={true} tone="sand">

Some of this reaches a long way past the plateau, and it tends to begin the same way it does here — with one friendship that kept going. A school of around 500 children in western Kenya began with a teacher from Kodera who came to Issaquah to study in 1999, and it’s still going.

A few of the partners we’re connected to:

- [**Pine Lake Academy**](/church-life/pine-lake-academy/) (Kodera, Kenya) — a preschool-through-8th-grade school founded in partnership with Pine Lake and funded, in part, by our donations over the last twenty years.
- [**Melinda Anderson**](https://giving.covchurch.org/personnel-detail/melinda-anderson) (Guatemala) — a Covenant partner working in education and community care, walking with people as they rebuild their lives.
- [**Evens & Karina Paul**](https://giving.covchurch.org/personnel-detail/EvensandKarinaPaul) (Haiti) — Covenant partners in rural Haiti who run a school of more than 350 students, pastor churches they’ve planted, and help families build sustainable livelihoods.

</Split>

<Split image="/assets/images/518360506_1167866075381380_870034891620119747_n.jpg" alt="An older volunteer and a young girl serving communion together" heading="Practicing generosity" eyebrow="Generosity" reverse={false} tone="paper">

Generosity here isn’t only money. Sunday mornings run on it: the coffee, the welcome at the door, the kids’ rooms and the music all happen because people bring what they have. Whatever you’re good at, there’s probably a use for it here.

Giving is the other half of it — one way we respond to God’s generosity toward us, and what keeps the lights on and the doors open: for our own gatherings, for the community groups who use the building through the week, and for the partners close to home and overseas.

[Learn more about giving at Pine Lake →](https://plcc.churchcenter.com/giving)

</Split>
