Most of what makes Pine Lake feel like home happens outside of Sunday — a smaller group that meets through the week, a shared meal, or the people who show up when a season turns hard.

- **Life Groups** — small groups that meet through the week to build friendships, study Scripture, and support one another.
- **Shared meals & gatherings** — regular rhythms like Tuesdays Together and MomCo that make it easy to find your people.

</Split>

<Split image="../../assets/images/church-life/481269178_1059869906180998_2732338907948607869_n.jpg" alt="The church gathered in matching shirts for the Global 6K for Water walk" heading="Serving others, here and far" eyebrow="Serving others" reverse tone="sand">

Following Jesus pulls us outward. Serving is woven into life at Pine Lake — close to home around Sammamish and Issaquah, and through long-term partners around the world.

- **Pine Lake Academy** (Kodera, Kenya) — a preschool-through-8th-grade school founded in partnership with Pine Lake and funded, in part, by our donations over the last twenty years.
- [**Melinda Anderson**](https://giving.covchurch.org/personnel-detail/melinda-anderson) (Guatemala) — a Covenant partner working in education and community care, walking with people as they rebuild their lives.
- [**Evens & Karina Paul**](https://giving.covchurch.org/personnel-detail/EvensandKarinaPaul) (Haiti) — Covenant partners in rural Haiti who run a school of more than 350 students, pastor churches they've planted, and help families build sustainable livelihoods.

[See how we serve our local community →](/neighbors/serve/)

</Split>
