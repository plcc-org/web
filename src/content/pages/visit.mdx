<Section eyebrow="When & where" heading="Sundays at 10:00am">

1715 228th Ave SE, Sammamish, WA 98075. There’s plenty of guest parking available, including accessible parking near the main entrance.

</Section>

<CaptionedPhoto image="/assets/images/pine-lake-exterior-2026.jpg" alt="The Pine Lake Covenant Church building in Sammamish, with its tall wooden cross" caption="Our building in Sammamish — look for guest parking near the main entrance." />

<Split image="/assets/images/719135962_18207937108338056_3061581680291060435_n.jpg" alt="The congregation standing in worship on a Sunday morning" heading="What happens on a Sunday" eyebrow="The morning" reverse={false} tone="paper">
  Our services last about **75 minutes** and include a mix of music, teaching, and time together.

  * Around **20 minutes of contemporary worship music**, led by a band — join in, or just listen
  * A short kids’ moment and a few brief Bible readings
  * A brief moment to say hi to the people around you
  * A **20–25 minute message** that’s thoughtful, practical, and grounded in the Bible

  Once a month, we also share **communion** together. You’re always free to participate — or not — as you feel comfortable.
</Split>

<Callout heading="Will I stand out or be put on the spot?">
  We won’t ask you to stand up and introduce yourself to the church, or raise a hand as a guest, and no one will point you out as new. There’s a brief meet-and-greet during the service, and we usually offer an icebreaker question to make it easy to say hello to someone nearby. Otherwise, join in as much as feels right — sing, pray, or simply take it in.
</Callout>

<Split image="/assets/images/658026969_18200890024338056_5693344916949832542_n.jpg" alt="A child happily playing in the Pine Lake Kids room" heading="We make Sundays smooth for parents" eyebrow="Bringing kids?" reverse={false} tone="sand">
  * Programs for children and youth of all ages, from **0–18**
  * **Quick check-in** in the foyer
  * All volunteers are **background checked**
  * A **secure pickup system** for children
  * At least **two adults** in every room
  * One Sunday a month, **K–5 students stay in the service**

  For babies and toddlers, there’s a comfortable space where you can **watch the service while caring for your child**. You can learn more on our [Kids & Families](/kids/) page.
</Split>

<Section eyebrow="A few practical things" heading="What should I wear?">

Whatever you’re comfortable in. On any given Sunday you’ll see everything from **Seahawks jerseys and jeans** to **smart casual**. There’s no dress code, and no expectations.

</Section>

<Closing eyebrow="No test required" heading="A place to belong">
  Some people who come to Pine Lake are deeply rooted in their faith. Others are exploring, returning after time away, or connected through family or community. All are welcome — you’ll never be asked what you believe to be welcome here.

  If you’d like to connect with someone ahead of time, you’re always welcome to — [say hello](/contact/) — but there’s no expectation that you do.
</Closing>
