On Sundays, we offer programming for children and youth of all ages, from babies through high school. Parents are always welcome to keep their children with them, or to use the programs that best fit their family.

- Programs available for **ages 0–18**
- **Quick check-in** in the foyer
- Consistent, caring volunteers who love kids
- A comfortable space where parents can **watch the service while caring for babies and toddlers**

One Sunday each month, our elementary-aged kids stay in the service to worship alongside their families.

We don’t see kids and youth as something separate happening down the hall. They’re an important part of the life of the church, and we love finding ways for families to worship and grow together.

</Split>
