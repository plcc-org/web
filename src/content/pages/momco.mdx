<Section eyebrow="What it is" heading="Good company">

Being a mom means being needed all day — and it can still be lonely. MomCo (short for Mom Community) is the counterweight: a couple of mornings a month that are about you, not your to-do list.

Pine Lake’s group is part of [The MomCo](https://www.themom.co/) — the organization you may remember as MOPS — and moms come with newborns, toddlers, and kids well into school, from Pine Lake, other churches, and no church at all. If you’re a mom with a kid at home, of any age, this is for you.

</Section>

<Video url="https://vimeo.com/994189284" title="Mom Invite — a one-minute video from The MomCo" caption="Sixty seconds from the national MomCo on why moms keep showing up." />

<Split image="/assets/images/656838890_18200890015338056_1357170169128708571_n.jpg" alt="A volunteer playing with toddlers during a weekday morning at Pine Lake" eyebrow="Morning meetups" heading="Two Mondays a month" tone="paper">

Morning meetups run **9:45–11:30am**, two Mondays a month from mid-September through mid-May.

- A guest speaker or a craft, and honest conversation — no agenda beyond good company
- **Childcare during the meetup** — add it on when you register, and the kids play down the hall while you finish a sentence
- Coffee you get to drink while it’s still hot, which regulars will tell you is the whole point

The full calendar of 2026–27 dates is on the [registration page](https://plcc.churchcenter.com/registrations/events/3743770).

</Split>

<Callout heading="Try two meetups first">

You’re welcome at your first two morning meetups before registering for anything. Come once, come back if it felt right, and decide after that — nobody will chase you with a form.

</Callout>

<Section eyebrow="Registration" heading="Membership for 2026–27">

Membership is **$150 for the year** — **$125 if you register by August 31**. That one fee covers everything above: childcare during meetups, speakers, crafts, playdates, moms’ nights out, and the magazine.

Cost should never be the thing that keeps you away. Scholarships and payment plans are available — email [the coordinators](mailto:mopscoordinator@plcc.org) and they’ll sort it out quietly.

</Section>
