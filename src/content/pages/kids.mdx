<Section eyebrow="Getting started" heading="Your first Sunday">

If you’re visiting for the first time, look for the **Kids Check-in** in the foyer. One of our volunteers will help you register your children and show you to their classrooms.

</Section>

<Callout heading="Safety is our priority">

We know that trust is earned. To ensure your child’s safety and your peace of mind, we follow strict protocols:

- **Background checks:** Every volunteer undergoes a comprehensive background screening.
- **Two-adult rule:** We maintain at least two background-checked adults in every classroom.
- **Secure check-in:** Our digital check-in system ensures that only authorized adults can pick up your child.

</Callout>

<Section eyebrow="Worshipping together" heading="Special Sundays">

Once a month, our elementary students (K–5) join the adults in the main service for **Family Sunday**. We believe it’s important for kids to see what it looks like to worship as a whole community. Don’t worry — the service is designed with families in mind, and we provide “busy bags” to help keep little hands occupied!

</Section>
