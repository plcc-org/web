Becca grew up as a missionary kid in the Democratic Republic of the Congo,
finished high school in Bellingham, and studied at Seattle Pacific University
before earning a master's in Theological and Biblical Studies from Fuller
Seminary. She joined Pine Lake in 2022 to work with our students, and in 2024
the church called her as an associate pastor. She also speaks at women's
retreats.

She and her husband, Rob, met when they were twelve and have been married more
than twenty years. They have two kids, Annabelle and Bradley, and a golden
retriever, Kaylee, who is counted firmly as a third. What gets her up in the
morning is helping people discover they're loved by God and learn to live
rooted in that love. And when she isn't working or parenting, there's a good
chance she's out on a trail — or in a coffee shop, drinking coffee, making
coffee, or meeting someone for coffee.
