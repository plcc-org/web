For the morning the building is closed: snow, a power cut, anything that changes whether
people should come.

## Steps

1. In the editor (/admin), open **Site notice**.
2. Write one short sentence in **Notice**, like “Sunday’s service is canceled because of
   snow.”
3. If there’s more to read, fill in **Links to (optional)**: a page on this site like
   `/events/`, or a full `https://` address. **Link text (optional)** defaults to “Details”.
4. Tick **Show this notice on every page**, and **Save**.

**Live in:** a few minutes, not seconds. Saving rebuilds the site, and only one rebuild runs
at a time, so one already in the queue goes first. **If a closure is decided the night
before, post it the night before.**

## Taking it down

Untick **Show this notice on every page** and save. The sentence can stay in the field,
ready for next time.

## Good to know

- It shows as a strip across the top of every page, the Sunday links page included, and
  stays until someone switches it off.
- While it’s on, it replaces the “Live now” banner (below).

## The “Live now” banner

The same strip is used on Sunday mornings to point to the livestream. It isn’t something
you edit. It shows on the home page only, from 9:50 to 11:30 on Sundays, and links to
the YouTube livestream. To see what it looks like on any other day, open the home page with
[?banner=on](/?banner=on) on the end of the address.
