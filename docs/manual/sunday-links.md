The tags and QR codes around the building open [/links/](/links/): this Sunday’s links at the
top, and the ones that never change in groups beneath. It’s the one page on the site for
people who are already in the room, so it’s a plain list, made to read on a phone.

Each Sunday is its own entry under **Sunday links**, named for its date. The page shows the
most recent Sunday on or before today, so you can get next week ready any time.

## Steps

1. In the editor (/admin), open **Sunday links**, then this week’s entry.
2. Choose **Duplicate**, and change the **Sunday** date to next Sunday. The entry’s name
   follows the date.
3. Add, remove or drag links into order, top to bottom as they’ll appear. Each one has
   **Link text** and **Links to**:
   - a website, as the full `https://` address, copied from the browser’s address bar
   - an email, as `mailto:` followed by the address
   - a page on this site, starting with `/`, like `/events/`
4. **Save.**

**Live:** early that Sunday morning, on its own, with the site’s nightly rebuild at about
5am. If someone saves anything on the Sunday itself, it goes live with that save.

## Seeing it before Sunday

- **In the editor**, a week that isn’t live yet previews beside its form.
- **Anywhere else**, open [/links/?preview](/links/?preview). A banner says it’s a preview
  and when it goes live. A save shows up there a few minutes later, once the site has
  rebuilt.

## The groups that never change

The groups beneath (“Next steps”, “Additional resources”) are under **Sunday links: every
week**: one entry holding every group, in order, each with its links in order. Change them
there, and they change for every week.

## Good to know

- **Old weeks delete themselves.** Each night, weeks before the live one are cleared, so
  the list only ever holds this week and the ones being prepared. With the old ones gone,
  the earliest date in the list is the live one.
- **Links open in the same tab** on this page, unlike the rest of the site, so someone
  working down the list on a phone can tap Back and land on it again.
- **If the nightly rebuild ever fails**, the page still switches over on the Sunday: next
  week’s links are already on it, waiting for the date.

## If something goes wrong

- **Two entries have the same date.** They can’t: nothing could tell which one was meant,
  and the site stops updating until it’s fixed. If you’re told a date is taken, or changes
  stop appearing after you’ve added a week, check that your copy has its new date.
- **A link won’t save.** The form says what it expects; usually it’s a missing `https://`
  or `mailto:`.
