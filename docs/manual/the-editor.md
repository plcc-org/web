The website’s editor is a tool called TinaCMS. It’s where every change to the site’s words,
pages and photos is made.

## Where it is

Add **/admin** to the website’s address: **plcc.dev/admin** while the new site is being
prepared.

## Logging in

You need an editor account first. Ask whoever looks after the site to add you, and you’ll
get an email inviting you in.

1. Go to /admin and choose **Log in**. A sign-in window opens.
2. Sign in with your email and password, or with **Authenticate With GitHub** if that’s how
   your account was set up. **Forgot your password?** is underneath if you need it.
3. Choose **Enter Edit Mode** if the editor asks you to.

![The TinaCloud sign-in window, with a GitHub button, then email and password fields](/manual-shots/sign-in.webp)

Keep your login to yourself. If someone else needs to edit, ask for an account for them.

## Finding your way around

The **☰** menu at the top left lists everything you can edit. Most of what you’ll change is
under **Pages**; the rest hold the smaller things the site shows.

![The editor’s menu, listing Pages, Sunday links, Site notice, Leadership and the other lists beside the list of pages](/manual-shots/menu.webp)

| In the menu                                            | What it holds                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Pages**                                              | The site’s pages ([Change some content](./change-some-words.md), [Add a page](./add-a-page.md))                                   |
| **Sunday links**, **Sunday links: every week**         | The links people open in the room on a Sunday ([Prepare Sunday’s links](./sunday-links.md))                                       |
| **Site notice**                                        | The banner for a closure ([Post a closure notice](./post-a-closure-notice.md))                                                    |
| **Leadership**, **Youth moments**, **Homepage quotes** | The staff, the youth year’s trips, and the homepage’s quotes ([Update staff, quotes and youth moments](./update-shared-lists.md)) |
| **Photo descriptions**                                 | What each photo shows, for people who can’t see it ([Add a photo](./add-a-photo.md))                                              |
| **Short links**                                        | Addresses like plcc.org/camp ([Add a short link](./add-a-short-link.md))                                                          |

In **Pages**, a folder holds the pages under one section, like **about**. The home page is
listed by its title, “Discovering life with Jesus together”.

## Editing a page

Open a page and the form sits on the left with the page itself on the right. Click anything
on the page and the block it belongs to opens in the form; as you type, the page updates.

![Plan a Visit open in the editor: the form on the left with its Title, Top of page and the list of blocks in Body, and the page on the right](/manual-shots/editing-a-page.webp)

- **Top of page** is the title, intro and photo at the very top.
- **Body** is the rest of the page, as a list of blocks: drag one by its handle to move it,
  or use **+** to add one. [Which block do I use?](./blocks.md) explains what they are.
- **Save** is at the bottom of the form, and **Reset** throws away changes you haven’t
  saved.

Saving publishes. The change is live a few minutes later, once the site has rebuilt.
