A page’s **Body** is a stack of blocks. Each one is a ready-made section, so whatever you
build stays in keeping with the rest of the site. Add one with the **+** on the Body field;
it lands at the bottom, so drag it up into place.

## Pick by what you have

Start at the top and take the first match.

| If what you have is…                               | Reach for                               |
| -------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| A few paragraphs that just need to be read         | [Text](#text)                           |
| Something better _shown_ than described            | [Photo beside text](#photo-beside-text) |
| One point you don’t want people to skim past       | [Callout](#callout)                     |
| One sentence someone said, worth its own space     | [Quote](#quote)                         |
| Three or four parallel things, each a line or two  | [Text cards](#text-cards)               |
| Three or four places to go next                    | [Link cards](#link-cards)               |
| A sequence where the order matters                 | [Roadmap](#roadmap)                     |
| A set of principles where the order doesn’t        | [Key points](#key-points)               |
| A single photo that needs explaining               | [Photo](#photo)                         |
| A minute of video that says it better than a page  | [Video](#video)                         |
| A moment of visual breathing room                  | [Photo gallery](#photo-gallery)         |
| The one thing you want the reader to do at the end | [Closing banner](#closing-banner)       |
| A personal note in someone’s own voice             | [Letter](#letter)                       |
| A short note that needs a logo beside it           | [Note with logo](#note-with-logo)       |
| Cards where a logo is the identity, not a photo    | [Logo cards](#logo-cards)               |
| “What’s coming up” that should stay current itself | [Featured events](#featured-events)     |
| The youth year’s trips and retreats                | [Youth moments](#youth-moments)         |
| Voices of the church, rotating                     | [Quotes carousel](#quotes-carousel)     |

## Rules of thumb

- **Order matters → Roadmap. Order doesn’t → Key points.** They look alike, but numbering
  things that aren’t a sequence tells the reader something untrue about how to read them.
- **Cards are for parallel things.** If your three cards aren’t the same _kind_ of thing,
  they should be prose.
- **Callout or Note with logo?** A Callout is a heading over text with a green accent rule,
  for one point that must land. A Note with logo is a compact tinted panel with room for a
  logo beside the text, and no heading.
- **One Closing banner per page, and it goes last.** It’s the loudest block, and it closes
  flush against the footer. The form won’t save a page with two, or with one that isn’t last.
- **Two Photo beside text blocks in a row need different backgrounds**, or they read as one
  long block.

## The blocks

### Text

![A preview of the Text block](/block-previews/Section.webp)

A few paragraphs that just need to be read. The default for written content.

### Photo beside text

![A preview of the Photo beside text block](/block-previews/Split.webp)

Something better shown than described: a photo beside a heading and a few paragraphs.
Choose which side the photo sits on, and a **Background** (Warm sand, White or Dark green).

### Callout

![A preview of the Callout block](/block-previews/Callout.webp)

One point you don’t want people to skim past, boxed with its own heading.

### Photo

![A preview of the Photo block](/block-previews/CaptionedPhoto.webp)

A single photo that needs explaining, with a caption underneath. The caption can say what
the photo can’t, like where to park.

### Video

![A preview of the Video block](/block-previews/Video.webp)

A YouTube or Vimeo video that says it better than a page. Paste the address from the
browser’s address bar, not the embed code.

### Closing banner

![A preview of the Closing banner block](/block-previews/Closing.webp)

The one thing you want the reader to do at the end. A dark band, one per page, and last.

### Photo gallery

![A preview of the Photo gallery block](/block-previews/PhotoBand.webp)

A moment of visual breathing room: several photos together, with few or no words.

### Text cards

![A preview of the Text cards block](/block-previews/CardRow.webp)

Three or four parallel things, each a short title and a line or two.

### Link cards

![A preview of the Link cards block](/block-previews/LinkCards.webp)

Three or four places to go next, each card a link to another page.

### Quote

![A preview of the Quote block](/block-previews/Quote.webp)

One thing someone said, worth its own space: a testimonial, a quotation, a verse. Say who
said it, like “A recent visitor”, or give the Bible reference.

### Featured events

![A preview of the Featured events block](/block-previews/FeaturedEvents.webp)

A short list of upcoming events that keeps itself current, from
[What’s On](./get-an-event-on-whats-on.md). Choose a category, or all of them, and how many
to show; three suits most pages.

### Key points

![A preview of the Key points block](/block-previews/KeyPoints.webp)

A set of principles where the order doesn’t matter, each a title and a line or two.

### Logo cards

![A preview of the Logo cards block](/block-previews/LogoCards.webp)

Cards each topped by a logo, with a line of text and an optional link. Each logo needs a
description of what it says or shows.

### Note with logo

![A preview of the Note with logo block](/block-previews/Aside.webp)

A short note in a tinted panel, with room for a logo beside it.

### Youth moments

![A preview of the Youth moments block](/block-previews/YouthMomentsBlock.webp)

The youth year’s trips and retreats, from the shared Youth moments list. Edit the list,
not the block: [Update staff, quotes and youth moments](./update-shared-lists.md).

### Quotes carousel

![A preview of the Quotes carousel block](/block-previews/QuoteCarousel.webp)

Voices of the church, rotating, from the shared Homepage quotes list.

### Roadmap

![A preview of the Roadmap block](/block-previews/Roadmap.webp)

Steps where the order matters, numbered along a line.

### Letter

![A preview of the Letter block](/block-previews/Letter.webp)

A personal note in someone’s own voice, beside their portrait and closing with a signature
(**Signed by**, **Their role**).

## A page, block by block

The [Plan a Visit](/visit/) page is the one a first-time guest actually reads. Here is why
each block is the one it is.

| Block                 | On the page                               | Why this one                                                                                             |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| _(top of page)_       | “Plan a Visit” and a reassuring intro     | The intro does the emotional work before any logistics.                                                  |
| **Text**              | “Sundays at 10:00am”: when and where      | Facts someone may be scanning for. Prose, not a card: they need to be _read_, and cards invite skimming. |
| **Photo**             | The building, captioned                   | “What am I looking for when I arrive?” A caption can say the thing a photo can’t.                        |
| **Photo beside text** | “What happens on a Sunday”                | Show and tell: the description is more believable next to a photo of it happening.                       |
| **Callout**           | “Will I stand out or be put on the spot?” | The biggest fear, answered where it can’t be skimmed past. That’s what a Callout is for.                 |
| **Photo beside text** | “We make Sundays smooth for parents”      | Same pattern, second audience. White, then Warm sand, keeps the two from reading as one long block.      |
| **Text**              | “What should I wear?”                     | A short practical answer. It doesn’t need a photo or a box.                                              |
| **Photo gallery**     | Three photos, no words                    | Breathing room before the close, so the last impression is faces rather than logistics.                  |
| **Closing banner**    | “A place to belong” and the button        | One action, at the end, on a dark band so it reads as the page’s conclusion.                             |

The shape underneath: **reassure → orient → show → answer the fear → show again → practical
detail → breathe → invite.** Most pages for guests want roughly that arc. You don’t have to
follow it, but if a page feels flat, compare it with this one.
